# Bible Study Apps & Downloads

Some recommended electronic bibles that you could download or access on your device.

*Please note that these apps and the content that these apps provide are not affiliated with us. Use your own discretion when you browse or use their resources.*

## For Bible Reading

### You Version

Read online at [bible.com](https://www.bible.com/)

**Features:**

* Provide bibles in many international languages
* You can download your favourite bible translations and read it offline
* All bible translations are free (when you share your account details with 3rd parties)
* Includes a very wide variety of translations even ones that you would normally have to purchase
* Include audio bibles
* Capture notes within the app
* Jump to verse, e.g. type "Joh 1:1" to jumps directly to John 1:1

**Critique:**

* Social media first design. Bible is secondary feature.

Download the free app for:

* [Android](https://app.bible.com/app-android)
* [iOS](https://app.bible.com/app-ios)
* [Kindle Fire](http://www.amazon.com/YouVersion-Bible/dp/B004MC8CA2)

### Bible Gateway

Read online at [biblegateway.com](https://www.biblegateway.com)

**Features:**

* Provide bibles in many international languages
* Includes both free and commercial content
* Includes a very wide variety of translations
* Account creation is optional

**Critique:**

* Need active internet connection

Download the app for:

* [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.csnmedia.android.bg)
* [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id506512797?pt=578105&ct=bgapp_main_apppage&mt=8)
* [Kindle Fire](https://www.biblegateway.com/assets/images/appinfo/amazon48.png)

### Literal Word

Read online at [literalword.com](https://literalword.com/lsb/contents)

**Features:**

* Best UI for mobile bible app (as far as I know)
* Include ESV, KJV, LSB, NAS95
* Works without internet
* Best search feature of all mobile apps (as far as I know)
  * Jump to verse, e.g. type "Joh 1:1" to jumps directly to John 1:1
  * Graphically indicate spread of search term across the bible
* Build-in lexicon (bible dictionary)
* No international languages

**Critique:**

* Only English
* Limited set of bible translations

Download the free app for:

* [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.literalword.mobile_app&hl=en-US)
* [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/literal-word-bible-lexicon/id1439010388)

## Revised English Version

Read online at [revbible.com](https://revbible.com/bcuk)

**Features:**

* No online registration required (respect privacy)
* Works without internet
* Close to the original languages, yet in modern English style
* Faithful to the text regardless of traditional readings or interpretations
* Translators advise their choice to translate certain scriptures the way they did
* Extensive commentary elucidating many difficult passages
* Kept God's name *[(most modern bibles replaced God's name with Lord)](https://ofgod.info/name#yhwh)*
* Online videos
* Jump to verse, e.g. type "Joh 1:1" to jumps directly to John 1:1

Download the free app for:

* [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.goodbarber.revapp)
* [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rev-bible-app/id1381443899)

Or download as a [module for common bible apps](https://www.revisedenglishversion.com/expt)

## For Bible Study

### Logos

**Features:**

* Provide bibles in some international language
* Include some commercial bible translations like ESV, NLT, NKJV for free
* Other commercial bible translations can be purchased
* Split screen support (translation comparison)
* Cross references
* Bible dictionary (Lexicon)
* Notes
* Commentaries
* Jump to verse, e.g. type "Joh 1:1" to jumps directly to John 1:1

**Critique:**

* Advertisement popups
* Need active internet connection
* Slower than [Literal Word](#literal-word)

Download the app for [Android, iOS, Kindle Fire](https://www.logos.com/bible-app)

### Core Bible App

Read online at [corebible.app](https://corebible.app/)

**Features:**

* Best UI for a bible web app
* Account creation is optional
* Include Greek and Hebrew bibles with English popups
* Include Apocrypha
* Choice of English AICNT, LEB, ULT, ASV, KJV, RV
* AICNT = Neutral Translation with Annotations for Over 7,000 Textual Variants
* Extensive commentary elucidating many difficult passages
* Jump to verse, e.g. type "Joh 1:1" to jumps directly to John 1:1
* Save bookmarks and notes (if registered)

**Critique:**

* Lacks popular translations like ESV, NKJV, etc.
* Need active internet connection

### MySword

**Features:**

* No online registration required (respect privacy)
* Works without internet (after module download)
* Modules include: interlinear bibles, commentaries, dictionaries, books and graphics
  * Provide modules in international languages
  * Wide range of free modules in single app on Android
  * All modules are free
* Create notes or journal entries within the app
* Export/Import notes and module lists
* The software is [open source](https://github.com/topics/mysword) (you can inspect or change the source code if you like)
  * With a bit of effort, you can compile your own bible, commentaries, dictionaries, books or graphics and use it in your app

**Critique:**

* Need license for split-screen feature
* Ugly UI
* Rely on non-intuitive icons (difficult for beginners)

Download the app for:

* [Android](https://www.mysword.info/download-mysword/mysword)
