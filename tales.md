# Misleading Bible Tales

It may in certain cases even be misleading as it communicates the beliefs of the story teller instead of reality. Therefore, I urge you to [download](downloads.md) and study [the Word of God](index.md) and [make it part of your life](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/life) and seek the [truth](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/light).
