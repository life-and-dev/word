# The Biblical Gospel

What is the gospel, and how do biblical proclamations describe its message?

Examining Bible passages can clarify what is announced and how people are called to respond.

## False Gospel

Paul warns against a different gospel that distorts the gospel of Christ (Galatians 1:6-9). In Galatians, the dispute concerns people requiring circumcision and observance of the law for justification, or being counted righteous before God (Galatians 2:3-5,15-21, 5:2-4, 6:12-13). Paul argues that a person is justified through faith in Jesus Christ rather than works of the law, and that requiring the law as the basis of justification sets aside God's grace (Galatians 2:16,21).

Some Christians describe the gospel as the unbiblical teaching that we should *accept* ([belief](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/faith)) that *[God incarnated to save the world](https://son.ofgod.info/shema/concerns/salvation)*. This is also not the gospel that the prophets, John, Jesus and the apostles preached.

It is very important to proclaim the true gospel according to the Bible.

## Biblical Gospel

The Father promised David a descendant whose kingdom would be established (2 Samuel 7:12-16):

> When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, **I will raise up your offspring after you**, who shall come from your body, and **I will establish his kingdom**. He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever. I will be to him a father, and he shall be to me a son. When he commits iniquity, I will discipline him with the rod of men, with the stripes of the sons of men, but my steadfast love will not depart from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away from before you. And your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever before me. **Your throne shall be established forever**. — 2 Samuel 7:12-16 (ESV)

Paul presents the gospel as promised beforehand in the Scriptures, concerning God's Son, descended from David according to the flesh and declared Son of God in power by resurrection (Romans 1:1-4):

> Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus, called to be an apostle, set apart for **the gospel of God**, which he promised beforehand through His prophets in the holy Scriptures, concerning His son, who was **descended from David according to the flesh** and was declared to be **the Son of God in power** according to the Spirit of holiness **by his resurrection from the dead**, Jesus Christ our Lord, — Romans 1:1-4 (ESV)

Before his death and resurrection, Jesus proclaimed God's gospel:

> Now after John was arrested, Jesus came into Galilee, proclaiming **the gospel of God**, and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and **the kingdom of God** is at hand; **repent and believe in the gospel**.” — Mark 1:14-15 (ESV)

His first hearers should not be assumed to have understood the later events as the apostles proclaimed them after his resurrection (Mark 9:31-32; Luke 24:6-8). Paul summarises what he received and delivered:

> Now I would remind you, brothers, of **the gospel** I preached to you, which you received, in which you stand, and by which you are being saved, if you hold fast to the word I preached to you — unless you believed in vain. For I delivered to you as of first importance what I also received: that **Christ died for our sins in accordance with the Scriptures**, that he was buried, that **he was raised on the third day in accordance with the Scriptures**, — 1 Corinthians 15:1-4 (ESV)

Philip preached good news about God's kingdom and the name of Jesus Christ, and those who believed were baptised (Acts 8:12).

> But when they believed Philip as he preached good news about **the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ**, they were **baptized**, both men and women. — Acts 8:12 (ESV)

Peter proclaimed Jesus' works, death and resurrection, and that God made him both Lord and Christ:

> “Men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus of Nazareth, a man attested to you by God with mighty works and wonders and signs that God did through him in your midst, as you yourselves know— this Jesus, delivered up according to the definite plan and foreknowledge of God, **you crucified and killed** by the hands of lawless men. **God raised him up**, loosing the pangs of death, because it was not possible for him to be held by it.
>
> ...
>
> This Jesus **God raised up**, and of that we all are witnesses.
>
> ...
>
> Let all the house of Israel therefore know for certain that **God has made him both Lord and Christ**, this Jesus whom you crucified.”
>
> — Acts 2:22-24,32,36 (ESV)
Peter also proclaimed forgiveness through Jesus' name for everyone who believes in him, and identified Jesus as the one appointed by God to judge the living and the dead:

> And he commanded us to preach to the people and to testify that he is **the one appointed by God to be judge of the living and the dead**. To him all the prophets bear witness that **everyone who believes in him receives forgiveness of sins through his name**.” — Acts 10:42-43 (ESV)

Paul connects God's coming judgement through the appointed man with the assurance of his resurrection:

> The times of ignorance God overlooked, but now **he commands all people everywhere to repent**, because he has fixed a day on which **he will judge the world in righteousness by a man whom he has appointed**; and of this he has given assurance to all **by raising him from the dead**.” — Acts 17:30-31 (ESV)

Paul also speaks of judgement through Jesus Christ in relation to his gospel:

> on that day when, **according to my gospel**, God judges the secrets of men **by Christ Jesus**. — Romans 2:16 (ESV)

Judgement is part of these proclamations, though not every gospel summary mentions it.

## Gospel Scope

Scripture proclaims the good news of [the Kingdom of God](https://kingdom.ofgod.info). It also calls people to respond, promises forgiveness and life, warns of judgement, and commissions disciples to make the message known.

### Message Proclaimed

1. **God and his kingdom:** Jesus identified the Father as the only true God and proclaimed the gospel of the [kingdom of God](https://kingdom.ofgod.info). He called people to repent and trust ([believe](faith.md)) in his Father's word. After Jesus' resurrection, his apostles continued to proclaim the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ (Matthew 4:17,23; Mark 1:14-15; John 12:49-50, 17:3; Acts 8:12, 28:30-31).
2. **Jesus the Messiah and Son:** Jesus is the Son of God, the promised descendant of David, and the Messiah ([Christ](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/christ)). God sent him and anointed him with the Holy Spirit and power (Romans 1:1-4; Acts 10:36-38).
3. **His life and works:** Jesus went about doing good and healing, and God attested him by mighty works, wonders and signs (Acts 2:22, 10:38-39).
4. **His death, burial and resurrection:** Christ died for our sins in accordance with the Scriptures, was buried and was raised on the third day. He appeared to witnesses after his resurrection (1 Corinthians 15:3-8).
5. **His exaltation and return:** God exalted Jesus to his right hand and made him Lord and Christ. Jesus will return from heaven (Acts 2:32-36; 1 Thessalonians 1:9-10, 4:16).

### Response Commanded

- **Repentance and faith:** Jesus calls people to repent and believe the gospel. The apostles proclaimed repentance towards God and faith in Jesus Christ (Mark 1:15; Acts 20:21).
- **Baptism and obedience:** Those who believed Philip's message were baptised. Jesus commanded his disciples to baptise and teach new disciples to observe his commands. Paul described his mission as bringing about the obedience of faith (Acts 8:12; Matthew 28:18-20; Romans 1:5).

### Consequences and Hope

- **Grace and forgiveness:** All have sinned. God offers salvation by grace through faith, not as a reward for works. Everyone who believes in Jesus receives forgiveness of sins through his name (Romans 3:23-24; Ephesians 2:8-10; Acts 10:43).
- **The Holy Spirit:** Peter called his hearers to repent and be baptised for forgiveness and spoke of the gift of the Holy Spirit. Scripture also records believers receiving the Holy Spirit before water baptism (Acts 2:38-39, 10:44-48).
- **Judgement:** God commands all people to repent and has appointed a day to judge the world in righteousness through the man he raised from the dead. Paul connects this judgement through Jesus Christ with his gospel (Acts 17:30-31; Romans 2:16).
- **Resurrection and life:** Christ's resurrection is the basis for the hope that those who belong to him will be raised at his coming. God gives eternal life through his Son (1 Corinthians 15:20-23; John 3:16-17).

### Commission to Proclaim

Jesus commissioned his disciples to make disciples of all nations, baptising them and teaching them to observe his commands. Scattered believers proclaimed the word, and believers are instructed to be ready to give an answer for their hope with gentleness and respect (Matthew 28:18-20; Acts 8:4; 1 Peter 3:15).

## Conclusion

The [biblical gospel](#biblical-gospel) announces God's kingdom and his work through Jesus Christ. Its [scope](#gospel-scope) includes the message proclaimed, the response commanded and its consequences, without reducing every proclamation to one fixed list. Galatians identifies a [distortion of the gospel](#false-gospel) concerning justification and the law, not merely a difference in wording.
